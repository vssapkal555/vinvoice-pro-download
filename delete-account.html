Delete your account and cloud data

Last updated: 6 September 2026

You can request deletion of your VInvoice Pro account and the cloud recovery data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email vinvoicepro@gmail.com from the email address registered to your VInvoice Pro account.
  2. Use the subject VInvoice Pro account deletion request.
  3. Do not send your password, invoice contents or other sensitive business information.
  4. Respond to any reasonable identity-verification request needed to protect the account.

What will be deleted

After verification, the registered VInvoice Pro authentication account and its associated cloud recovery record will be scheduled for deletion, subject to information that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legal obligations.

What remains on your device

Deleting the online account does not automatically erase the local VInvoice Pro database, exported PDFs, spreadsheets or files already saved or shared from your device. Use Android app-data controls or uninstall VInvoice Pro to remove its local app data, and separately delete exported files where you saved them.

Before requesting deletion

Export any records you are legally required to retain. Account and cloud-data deletion may be irreversible and can prevent future cloud restoration.

Contact

Deletion and privacy requests: vinvoicepro@gmail.com
