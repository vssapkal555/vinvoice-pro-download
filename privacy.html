Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 6 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the VInvoice Pro Android application handles information when you create an account, use offline invoicing tools, or use cloud backup and recovery.

Information handled by VInvoice Pro

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register, plus authentication information handled by Supabase Auth. VInvoice Pro does not display or store your password in its local business database.
  • Business information: information you enter for companies, customers, products, invoices, payments, expenses, notes, tax details and related records.
  • Cloud recovery information: when cloud backup is used, a supported snapshot of your VInvoice Pro business records and backup metadata is stored for your authenticated account.
  • Device preferences: settings such as theme, remembered email address, selected company and cloud-backup state may be stored locally on your device.
  • Support communications: information you choose to include when contacting support.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, provide invoicing and reporting features, remember device preferences, create and restore account-based recovery backups, respond to support requests, protect the service and comply with applicable law.

Local storage and cloud recovery

Day-to-day operational business data is stored in the app's local database on your device. Cloud recovery is a disaster-recovery copy and is not real-time multi-device synchronization. Restoring a backup can replace local VInvoice Pro data on that device after you confirm the action.

Service providers and disclosure

VInvoice Pro uses Supabase for account authentication and account-based cloud recovery storage. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect users, the service or legal rights. VInvoice Pro does not sell personal or business information and does not use it for third-party advertising.

Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, including authenticated access and database row-level access rules intended to restrict cloud recovery records to the relevant account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on a device until it is removed through app use, Android app-data controls or uninstalling the app. Cloud recovery information may remain until it is replaced or deleted. To request deletion of your VInvoice Pro account and associated cloud recovery data, follow the account and data deletion instructions.

Your choices

You control the business information entered into the app, whether to create or replace a cloud recovery backup, and when to export or share documents. You may contact support to ask about, correct or request deletion of account-related information.

Children's privacy

VInvoice Pro is a business invoicing application and is not directed to children.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the application, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published.
